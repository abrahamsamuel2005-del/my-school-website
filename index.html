About Triumph Heritage Academy

Established on 10 September 2012, Triumph Heritage Academy, Marmara, is committed to providing a safe, inclusive and stimulating learning environment where every child can discover their potential and grow.

We believe that education goes beyond the classroom. We are committed to developing the whole child—academically, socially, emotionally and morally—while creating a foundation for lifelong learning and meaningful achievement.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of excellence where every child is inspired to learn, equipped to excel, and prepared to make a positive impact in an ever-changing world.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, inclusive and stimulating learning environment where every child is nurtured to discover their potential, develop strong character, acquire excellent academic and life skills, and grow into confident, responsible and productive members of society.

Our Motto

“EXCELLENT LEARNING FOR PROGRESS”

Admissions

Triumph Heritage Academy welcomes applications for children from the age of 3 years.

We provide admission opportunities for learners in Pre-Nursery and Primary 1–4.

Admission Requirements

  • Child must be at least 3 years old.
  • Birth certificate.
  • Recent passport photograph.
  • Medical fitness certificate.
  • Transfer certificate, where applicable.

Admission Process

1. Enquiry

Contact the school to enquire about available classes, admission requirements and other relevant information.

2. Application

Complete the admission process and provide the required documents.

3. Assessment

Where applicable, the school may assess the learner before admission.

4. Admission

Successful applicants will receive information about completing the enrolment process.
